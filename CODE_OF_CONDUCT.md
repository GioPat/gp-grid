# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

gp-grid is maintained as a respectful, practical, and inclusive open source project. Everyone participating in the project is expected to help keep the community professional and welcoming.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Using clear, respectful language.
- Giving constructive technical feedback.
- Assuming good intent while still being direct about problems.
- Respecting different experience levels and perspectives.
- Keeping discussions focused on the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained unconstructive criticism.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated off-topic behavior.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, release comments, community chats, and any public or private interaction made in the context of gp-grid.

It also applies when someone represents the project publicly, such as by using an official project account or speaking on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior.

They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct.

Depending on the situation, maintainers may take actions including:

- A private or public clarification.
- A warning.
- Temporary limits on participation.
- Closing or locking discussions.
- Blocking or banning a participant from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the impact of the behavior and focused on protecting the health of the project community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainer

Reports should include relevant links, screenshots, usernames, dates, and a short description of what happened when possible.

Maintainers will handle reports with discretion and will not disclose private details beyond what is necessary to review and respond to the situation.
